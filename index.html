About

Not a resume.
A record.

I've built things from nothing — and made them matter. At 20, I walked into a Manhattan studio and learned commercial art from the ground up. At 28, I built the first digital delivery platform for pre-production art, two days before the world stopped, and incorporated the day after.

I'm equal parts creative and commercial. I've sketched storyboards and closed enterprise accounts. I've written backend code and cold-called strangers who became long-term clients. I've navigated bankruptcy, a decade of litigation, and market collapses — and I've rebuilt every time.

The throughline? I always find a way. I see the picture before it exists, and I don't stop until it does.

Career

The Long
Game.

Three decades across two industries, one constant: always building.

1993

GEM Studios

Illustrator → Senior Creative Director & Head of Sales · Manhattan, NY

Joined as a 20-year-old illustrator, hired out of an accelerated program at the Art Institute of Pittsburgh. Promoted to lead creative direction, new business development, and pre-production for one of Manhattan's most competitive commercial studios. Stayed eight years and left running the room.

2001

Green Light Studios Inc.

Founder & CEO — Solo · NYC Metro

Founded two days before 9/11. Incorporated the day after. Built the first digital delivery platform for commercial pre-production art — any art director, anywhere in the world, could access their storyboards remotely. Before cloud storage. Before Slack. Before remote work had a name.

Year 1: $200K. Year 2: $750K. Years 3–8: $3.5M–$4.3M annually. Solo founder — handled product development, backend build, UX/UI design, strategy, and all sales.

First to Market
2013

Green Light Studios LLC

Founder · NYC Metro

Restructured and continued operating through the post-2010 advertising market collapse. Adapted the business model as the industry shifted away from traditional pre-production workflows. Navigated one of the hardest periods of his life — professionally and personally — and came out the other side.

2014

T&T International → FedEx

Driver → Inside Sales → International Account Executive · Moonachie, NJ

Started over from scratch — literally driving delivery routes at night — and reached International Account Management in under five months. Grew a $1.7M portfolio to $2.25M in 18 months (27% growth). FedEx acquired T&T specifically for its dominant European freight network.

2019

Pierce Eagle Equipment

Account Executive — NJ/NY Territory

B2B sales of industrial pipe inspection, cleaning, and hydro-excavation equipment to municipalities, contractors, and utility teams across New Jersey and New York. Consultative selling for complex infrastructure needs; built long-term relationships with public sector clients.

2022

Hungate Mediation Services

Operations & Client Relations · Montclair, NJ

Managing all operations for a high-volume mediation practice — full calendar management, scheduling 12+ months ahead, processing legal documentation, coordinating proceedings, and maintaining client relationships at scale.

Now

Ball Barrier

Founder — Patent Pending

Designing and developing Ball Barrier — a patented solution that prevents balls from escaping beneath sports fencing. Built for parks, schools, municipalities, and sports facilities. Concept to prototype, driven by a decade of coaching competitive travel softball.
